Fried Eggs

Description

Fried Egg;
A recipe packed with nutrients, with an old style in cooking
Everybody in the family enjoys it with a dash of salt!
Served mainly during breakfast, this recipe is an all-time favorite to many of my friends and family.

Ingredients

  • 1 egg, whole
  • 1 pinch salt, iodized
  • 1 tsp cooking oil

Steps\Preparations( 5 mins| Cooking ~1min| Serves 1)

  1. Break the egg into a clean bowl.
  2. Heat the cooking oil in a pan.
  3. Whisk the egg to mixture.
  4. Add a pinch of salt to the mixture and whisk again.
  5. Pour the egg on the pan and fry for about 30 seconds. Then flip it and fry for another 30 seconds.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
